Images, backgrounds & links

This single-page example demonstrates image attributes, image alignment, a clickable image, page and paragraph backgrounds, a heading border, and custom hyperlink colors.

1 · Image attributes

This paragraph has its own background color and a soft background image. The image below uses src, descriptive alt, width, and height attributes.

2 · Align images left, center & right

Each image has a fixed display size; CSS margins place it at the left, center, or right of the content area.

3 · Image as a hyperlink

Click the photograph to open the Nature article. The whole image is inside an anchor tag.

Hyperlinks have custom default, visited, and hover colors. Try this sample link and move the pointer over it.
