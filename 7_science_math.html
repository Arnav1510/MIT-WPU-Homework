Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants make their food using sunlight.

The chemical equation for photosynthesis is:

6CO2 + 6H2O → C6H12O6 + 6O2

Einstein's Equation

Einstein's famous mass-energy equivalence equation is:
